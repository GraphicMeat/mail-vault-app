MailVault is a mail app that keeps your mail where it belongs: on your phone and on your own mail server. There is no MailVault account to create, no ads and no tracking. Your mail never passes through our servers.

Add Gmail, Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com, Fastmail and other JMAP services, or any IMAP account. Use several accounts side by side.

FREE
• A fast message list that stays smooth with thousands of messages
• Read, reply, forward and write mail, with drafts and attachments
• Search the mail on your phone, or ask the server for older mail
• Swipe to delete, move, flag or mark as read, with undo
• Mail you send offline waits in an outbox and goes out when the connection is back
• Remote images stay blocked until you choose to load them
• Notifications for new mail
• Delay a send by up to 5 minutes

PREMIUM (OPTIONAL SUBSCRIPTION)
• Scheduled send: pick the date and time, up to 24 hours ahead
• Follow-up reminders: get a reminder if nobody replies in 1, 3 or 7 days
• Tracker blocking: removes tracking pixels, so senders cannot tell you opened their mail

ABOUT NEW-MAIL NOTIFICATIONS
Android decides when MailVault may check for new mail in the background. In practice that is about every 15 minutes at best, and less often when the battery is low or you rarely open the app. Open MailVault to check right away. Scheduled messages and follow-up reminders also run when MailVault gets its turn, so they can be a little late.

YOUR PRIVACY
Your mail, passwords and sign-in tokens stay on your phone (passwords and tokens in Android secure storage). MailVault talks only to your mail provider. The only data we receive is what Premium needs: a random install ID and the purchase record from Google Play. If you sign in with your email address, we also keep that address.

MailVault Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly. The price is shown in the app before you buy. Payment is charged to your Google Account at confirmation and renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel it in your Google Account settings.
