Assistance in the inbox you already use

Google’s current Gmail help describes Gemini tools for summarizing threads, drafting and refining mail, finding details, organizing messages, and scheduling. Feature availability requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan, with language support and other account conditions. Check the account you actually use before expecting the full assistant. Official guidance ↗

A team already using Gmail, Drive, and Calendar may value the familiar interface more than a new client’s layout. Test a task that crosses a real boundary: ask about an email, open the original attachment, and confirm the related calendar event. The useful outcome is arriving at the right source and action, not simply receiving a fluent answer in a side panel.

An inbox assistant is not an independent archive

Gemini’s inbox assistance works within Google’s service. Google’s product page shows it helping with replies, writing, questions, and summaries. If your main concern is retaining records after an account change, evaluate the export and recovery path separately from those AI features. Google integration makes the assistant convenient; it does not replace a deliberate local preservation workflow. Official guidance ↗

Google provides a data-download route for exporting account data. For email, check the actual export selection and inspect the result before changing or closing an account. A one-time export and an everyday client with local mail are different operating routines: decide how often you need to refresh the archive and how you will search it later. Official guidance ↗

Pricing and availability

Gmail has free core use; Gemini availability and cost depend on an eligible Google AI or Workspace plan and region. Official guidance ↗

Why MailVault is our choice for local ownership

MailVault connects to your existing email and saves chosen messages as .eml files on your disk. You can search that archive locally, read originals offline once saved, and make manual backups for free. Its AI writing and summaries support on-device models, and Gmail message processing is restricted to on-device providers. Premium adds scheduled backups and tools for managing retained mail, making local ownership a daily workflow.

Three checks before you switch

  1. Check Gemini eligibility, language support, and account settings.
  2. Use a known thread to verify the answer against the original messages.
  3. Save and back up a sample folder, then find its oldest message offline.

For MailVault, offline access covers messages and attachments already saved. Sending and synchronization need a connection. Keep a second backup on another disk. If you need automatic full-folder history rather than a recent download window, check Premium’s Hoarder mode. Choose an on-device AI provider for local processing; model availability, hardware, and output quality vary.
