Questions About Pricing

Why is there a subscription at all?

MailVault is a real product built and maintained by a small team, not a side project living on goodwill. The subscription funds ongoing development, bug fixes, and the servers behind sync and billing for Premium features. The core app doesn't need a server to work, so it stays free forever — Premium exists to pay for the parts that do.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. On the yearly plan, cancel during the 14-day trial and you won't be charged. Cancel after that and Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, then the account quietly drops back to Free — nothing is deleted or locked.

Will my local data ever be gated behind a paywall?

No. Reading, composing, searching, and archiving your email locally is free forever and always will be. Premium only gates automation, export and tracker blocking on top — scheduling, migration, cleanup rules, Time Capsule snapshots, image/HTML export, and stripping tracking pixels out of incoming mail. Your .eml files on disk are yours regardless of subscription status.

What happens to backups I already made manually?

They stay exactly where they are. Manual, one-click archiving is a Free feature — it always has been and it isn't changing. Premium just adds the option to schedule that same archiving automatically instead of clicking the button yourself.

Where do I actually subscribe?

Inside the app. Download MailVault for free, then go to Settings → Billing to start the yearly 14-day trial or subscribe monthly. There's no checkout on this website.
