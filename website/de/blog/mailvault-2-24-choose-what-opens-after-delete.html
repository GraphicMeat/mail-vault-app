MailVault 2.24: Choose What Opens After You Delete

4 Min. Lesezeit

MailVault 2.24 is out. The headline for keyboard users: you now choose what opens when you delete the email you are reading. It came straight from a feature request on our Discord, and it is the part of this release we want to explain properly.

The request

A user of the Mac app posted in our #requests channel with a problem. Deleting an email left a blank reading pane saying "Select a message to read", and no email was selected in the list. Pressing j or k selected the top email in the inbox, not the one next to the email just deleted.

For someone who works through a mailbox with the keyboard, that is a stop at every delete: reach for the mouse, or start again from the top. The request was to open another email automatically, and, better, to let you choose which one. There are several sensible answers: the next or the previous email, the next or the previous unread email, or an email from the same account as the one you deleted, which matters when you read All Inboxes and the neighbour in the list belongs to another account.

MailVault already had a setting for what happens after a delete, but it only covered part of this. The suggestion was a better design, so we built it.

What you can choose now

Open Settings → Mail preferences → Behavior → Deleting and look at When an email is deleted:

  • Select nothing (the default) closes the reading pane, as before.
  • Select the next email opens the email below the deleted one.
  • Select the previous email opens the one above it.
  • Select the next unread email and Select the previous unread email open the nearest unread email below or above.

Once you pick an option that opens an email, a Stay in the same account switch appears. In a list that spans several accounts, such as All Inboxes, it prefers an email from the account of the one you deleted. MailVault gives up "unread" before it gives up "same account". With no unread email left, or none left in that account, the nearest email opens instead of an empty pane.

Two details we fixed on the way. With Select nothing, pressing j or k now continues from where the deleted email was, rather than starting again at the top. And deleting in All Inboxes no longer ends with "Delete failed: Native refresh failed" when the delete worked and only one account could not be refreshed afterwards: the list repaints with what did arrive, and Undo is still offered.

The requester tried it on our nightly update track the day after and came back happy, with the same-account switch as the favourite part. If you want to try early changes yourself, nightly builds are a choice in Settings → Software updates.

The full walkthrough, with each option explained, is in the Getting Started FAQ, under "What opens after I delete an email?"

More in 2.24

  • Preview an attachment before you send it. A picture or PDF attached in compose has an eye button that opens it in the same preview as the reading pane.
  • Save a picture from a message. Right-click a picture and choose Save As, or Open in Preview, as before the new message renderer.
  • Delete all ticked emails. With several emails ticked, the delete key now deletes all of them, even with another email open in the reading pane.
  • A false Reply-To warning is gone. Receipts from some senders showed a "Reply-To domain mismatch" with a long garbled address, because MailVault read part of the sender's DKIM signature as the reply address.
  • Contacts has an All senders tab. Everyone who has emailed you, saved or not, searchable, with their emails one click away.
  • The phone app caught up. Offline mail you size per account, All Inboxes, grouped conversations, aliases, templates, the chat view, link safety, quiet hours, and more of the desktop settings, all brought over with the QR code setup.

Every change is listed in the changelog.

Tell us what is in your way

The best requests describe a moment that annoys you, as this one did: what happened, what you wanted instead, and why it matters for the way you work. Post yours in #requests on our Discord. Bugs go to #support.
