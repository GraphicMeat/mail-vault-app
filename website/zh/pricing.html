How do I start the trial?

Download and open MailVault, then go to Settings → Billing and choose Yearly. Complete checkout to start your 14-day trial. The trial is available for your first yearly subscription; returning subscribers should check their eligibility in the app.

When will I be charged?

The yearly subscription starts charging after the trial unless you cancel first. Monthly subscriptions are charged when you subscribe and do not include the yearly trial. Review the amount, payment details, and renewal date in checkout before confirming.

我可以随时取消吗？

You keep access to your saved mail and Free features. Premium remains active until the end of your trial or paid period, then the app returns to Free. Cancel during the yearly trial to avoid the subscription charge.

Why pay for Premium?

Premium adds scheduled backups, snapshots, migration, cleanup, export, and tracker blocking. Your subscription also supports ongoing development and maintenance by our small team. The full mail client and manual archiving remain free.

Does Premium upload my email to MailVault?

Your archived email stays in your local vault. Premium billing uses an online service to manage your subscription and devices; it is separate from your stored messages. Read how we handle data.

What if I already installed MailVault?

Open the app and go to Settings → Billing. Choose Yearly for the trial, if eligible, or Monthly. You do not need to download the app again.

Can I keep using my backups if I stop paying?

Yes. Your local files and Free features remain available. Premium automation stops after the trial or paid period ends; your existing archives and backups are not deleted.
