A MailStore Alternative for Mac: MailVault

6 分钟阅读

If you have searched for email archiving software, you have probably found MailStore Home. It is one of the most popular tools for archiving emails locally on Windows. The problem is that MailStore Home runs only on Windows 10 and 11, with no Mac version. If you are on macOS (or Linux), you need an alternative.

MailVault is a free email app for macOS, Windows and Linux that archives your mail to your own disk. It keeps a copy of Gmail, Microsoft 365, Outlook.com and IMAP mail on your Mac (Intel or Apple Silicon), stores it in standard open formats, and the core app costs nothing.

This page is about the Mac. For a head-to-head with the Windows edition, read MailVault vs MailStore Home. If you need an archive for a whole company, see MailStore Server alternatives, and for every option side by side, MailStore alternatives compared.

MailStore 做得好的地方

对 Windows 用户来说，MailStore Home 是个扎实的产品。公平地说说它的强项：

  • 成熟且经过充分检验 — MailStore 从 2006 年就有了。它对各种边角情况处理得不错，支持的邮件服务商也很广。
  • 全文搜索 — MailStore 会为归档的邮件建索引，让你在整份归档里做快速全文搜索。
  • 多种来源类型 — 除了 IMAP，MailStore 还能从 Outlook 的 PST 文件、Exchange 服务器和其他本地邮件客户端归档。
  • 企业版 — MailStore Server 面向需要集中式邮件归档的机构，带合规功能、审计轨迹和留存策略。

MailStore Server is aimed at small and medium-sized businesses and runs on Windows and Windows Server. For personal use on a Mac, neither edition is an option.

MailVault 强在哪里

它能在 Mac 上跑。这是最明显的差别。如果你用 macOS，MailStore 根本不是一个选项。MailVault 原生支持 macOS（Intel 和 Apple Silicon）以及 Linux。不需要 Wine，不需要虚拟机，也不需要任何变通办法。

Open standard storage. MailStore keeps your archived emails in its own archive files, and you export them to get EML or MSG files out. If MailStore stops being developed, accessing those emails becomes harder. MailVault stores every email as a standard .eml file, the RFC 5322 format that other email clients can open. Your archive is just a folder of files. You can open them in Apple Mail, Thunderbird, or any text editor.

Public source code. MailVault's source code is on GitHub. You can read it and check what it does with your mail. For something that handles your personal emails, knowing exactly what the software does matters.

它同时也是一个邮件客户端。MailStore 纯粹是归档工具 — 你归档完邮件，还得另外用一个客户端去读。MailVault 两样都是：你可以直接在应用里阅读、搜索、撰写、回复和转发邮件，想永久保留时再把它们归档到本地磁盘。

Local search, offline. MailVault builds a full-text index of the mail on your Mac and searches it without a connection. You can narrow by sender, date, folder or tag. In our published search test, the slowest search across 50,000 messages took 14 milliseconds.

How much stays on your Mac. By default MailVault keeps the last few months of mail on your computer, and you can choose 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. Mail you archive and your backups are never removed by that setting. Hoarder (Premium) keeps every folder and all history.

Archive, then delete, with a check. Archiving a message and deleting it from the server are free as separate steps. Archive & Delete (Premium) does both at once: it copies each message into your vault, checks the copy, and only then deletes it from the server.

MailStore 强在哪里

诚实地说说差距：

  • Attachment search for free: MailStore Home indexes attachments as well as messages at no cost. MailVault also has a local full-text index, but searching inside PDF and Office attachments is Premium.
  • PST and Exchange import: if you have Outlook PST files or need to archive from an Exchange server directly, MailStore supports these sources. MailVault connects over IMAP or Microsoft Graph and imports MBOX files, but it cannot read PST files.
  • Enterprise compliance: MailStore Server (the paid edition) offers centralized archiving, retention policies, audit trails, and legal hold features that organizations need for compliance. MailVault is designed for personal and small business use, not enterprise compliance.
  • Maturity: MailStore has been on the market since 2006, about 20 years. It has handled more edge cases, more email providers, and more user scenarios than MailVault has seen yet.

谁更该选 MailVault？

如果符合以下情况，MailVault 就是对的选择：

  • 你用 macOS 或 Linux，并且需要本地邮件归档。
  • 你希望归档的邮件保存为标准的开放格式（而不是专有数据库）。
  • You prefer free software whose source code you can read.
  • 你想要一个既是邮件客户端又是归档工具的应用 — 阅读、发送和归档都在一个地方。
  • 你是自由职业者、顾问或小企业主，需要一份个人邮件归档，但不想要企业级的复杂度。
  • 你想通过归档到本地再从服务器删除来腾出邮箱空间。

If you need compliance features for a company, read MailStore Server alternatives. If your old mail is in PST files, MailStore Home on Windows can read them. For personal email archiving on a Mac, MailVault does the job, and the core app is free.

其他替代方案

为完整起见，这里列出 Mac 用户有时会考虑的其他选项：

  • Apple Mail + local folders: you can drag emails into local mailboxes in Apple Mail, and Mailbox > Export Mailbox saves them in mbox format. There is no step that checks a local copy before you delete from the server, and no Maildir export.
  • Thunderbird: free and cross-platform. Its Archive button can move mail into Local Folders on your Mac, and the ImportExportTools NG add-on exports EML, mbox or PDF. Archiving is manual, and its local storage uses one mbox file per folder by default. See MailVault vs Thunderbird.
  • Google Takeout / provider export: a one-time bulk export to .mbox files. On its own it is not searchable or incremental, and .mbox is a single-file format. MailVault can import a Takeout .mbox file into your vault.
  • Mail Archiver X: a paid Mac app from Moth Software that archives Apple Mail, Outlook, Thunderbird and IMAP accounts into its own database and exports to PDF or MBOX. It and other tools are in seven MailStore alternatives compared.

MailVault fills the gap: an email app with a local archive for Mac, Windows and Linux, with open formats and a free core app.
