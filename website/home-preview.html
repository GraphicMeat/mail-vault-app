Privacy

Your inbox is nobody's business.
Not even ours.

Your email goes straight from your provider to your computer. It never passes through MailVault servers, so there is nothing for us to read, sell or train AI on.

  • Your mail lives on your diskRead and search it offline, in a folder you choose.
  • No account with usSign in with your provider. Passwords stay in your system keychain.
  • Know who really sent itSPF, DKIM and DMARC results shown right on the message.

Tracker blocking

Read in private.
Senders learn nothing.

Marketing emails hide tiny images that report when and where you open them. MailVault finds them in every message and warns you about links that lie about where they go.

  • See every trackerA clear marker on each message that tries to watch you.
  • Strip them before they load PremiumSenders are never told you opened it.
  • Catch lying linksA warning when the text of a link and its real destination do not match.

Backups

Free up your mailbox.
Keep every message.

Save the mail you still need, let MailVault check each copy, then make room on the server. Your archive is plain .eml files that open in other mail apps, even if MailVault disappeared tomorrow.

  • Unlimited manual backupsEvery account, every folder. No quota from us.
  • Checked before anything is deletedEach copy is verified on disk before the server copy goes.
  • Backups that run themselves PremiumDaily, weekly or your own schedule, with health checks.
  • Time Capsule PremiumBrowse a mailbox as it looked on any earlier date.

Customization

Email that fits the way you work.
Change almost anything.

Threads, chat bubbles or a timeline. Two columns or three. Your shortcuts, your toolbar, your theme. Set it up once, then change your mind whenever you like.

  • Pick how mail readsConversations, chat view, Explorer or grouped by sender.
  • Arrange your windowResizable 2 and 3 column layouts, light and dark themes.
  • Your keys, your actionsRebind shortcuts and choose which actions sit in the toolbar.
  • Only the alerts you wantNotification rules per account and per folder.

Coming from Notion Mail

Notion Mail is gone.
Your way of working isn't.

Notion Mail shut down on 22 September 2026. MailVault brings back what made it useful: inboxes built from your own views, fields on every message and rules that sort mail for you. It works with your Gmail account, and your mail stays on your computer.

  • Views that act like inboxesFilter by tag, sender, date or folder and group the result. Three views free, unlimited with Premium.
  • Fields on any messageOwner, priority, a due date or a tick box, kept on your computer.
  • Rules written in plain wordsDescribe what to catch and MailVault tags new mail as it arrives.
  • Free AI writing helpDraft, shorten or summarise with a model that runs on your computer.
