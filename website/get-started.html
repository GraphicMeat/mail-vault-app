Open MailVault.
You’re a few steps away.

No need to download again if you already have the app.

  1. Connect your email account

    Follow the account setup in the app. Gmail and Microsoft accounts use provider sign-in; other providers may need their IMAP settings.

  2. Make your first archive

    Select a few messages and choose Archive. Open a saved message and check its “Saved in your vault” status. Server copies stay in place.

  3. Keep going with Free

    Start your yearly trial

    Subscribe to monthly Premium

    Read, compose, reply, and archive whenever you need. If you want scheduled backups later, open Settings → Billing.

    Open Settings → Billing, choose Yearly, and complete checkout. Review trial eligibility, the renewal amount, and the payment details before confirming.

    Open Settings → Billing, choose Monthly, and complete checkout. Review the price and payment details before confirming.

Your local mail stays accessible if you cancel or return to Free.
