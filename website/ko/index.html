Start with a few messages

A copy you can see.
A cleanup you control.

Try archiving a small selection first. Your server copy stays in place until you choose to remove it.

  1. 1 Connect your email

    Add your existing account. Sign in with your provider; MailVault stores credentials in your system keychain.

  2. 2 Choose what to keep

    Select your messages and choose Archive. Copies are saved in your vault and stay on the server too.

  3. 3 Check your local copy

    Look for Saved in your vault, then open the message. When you’re ready to free space, use archive-then-delete to save and verify each copy before server removal.

More questions, answered →
Will connecting my account delete any email?

No. Connecting lets MailVault read and sync your mailbox. Choose Archive to save a copy while leaving the server copy in place. Server cleanup is a separate choice; Archive & Delete saves and verifies each copy before removing it from the server.

Can I keep using my current email app?

Yes. Use MailVault alongside your current app for archiving, or use its full mail client to read, compose, reply, and search every day.

Does syncing mean my mail is backed up?

Syncing keeps your mailbox view up to date. For a lasting local copy, archive the messages you want to keep. Manual archiving and backups are free; scheduled automatic backups are a Premium feature.

메일은 어디에 저장됩니까?

In the vault, a folder on your computer. Each archived message is a standard .eml file you can open in other mail apps. Keep a backup of that folder on another drive: local storage still depends on a working disk and available space.

Which email accounts can I connect?

Gmail, Microsoft 365, Outlook.com, and IMAP accounts, including many business email providers. See the account setup guide for your provider.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

You keep access to your saved mail and Free features. Premium remains active until the end of your trial or paid period, then the app returns to Free. Cancel during the yearly trial to avoid the subscription charge.
