How to Back Up Yahoo Mail to Your Computer

6 min read

Yahoo Mail has changed ownership and infrastructure several times over the years, and its account security options have shifted along with it. A lot of older backup tutorials tell you to generate an "app password" for Yahoo and use it to connect a mail client over IMAP. That advice is now out of date — Yahoo Mail does not support app-specific passwords the way Gmail or iCloud do. If a tool is asking you to generate one for Yahoo specifically, it's following an outdated flow that won't work for most accounts today.

What actually works for Yahoo Mail

Yahoo still fully supports IMAP access for third-party mail clients — you just connect through Yahoo's standard sign-in flow instead of an app password. In practice, that means:

  • IMAP server: imap.mail.yahoo.com, port 993, SSL/TLS.
  • SMTP server (if you're also sending): smtp.mail.yahoo.com, port 465 or 587, SSL/TLS.
  • Sign-in: use your regular Yahoo account credentials through the client's normal connection flow. Depending on your account's security settings, Yahoo may prompt you to approve the new device or client the first time.

If your mail client specifically insists on an app password and Yahoo's account settings no longer offer a way to generate one, that client's Yahoo support may simply be broken or outdated — it's not something wrong with your account.

Why back up Yahoo Mail at all

Yahoo gives free accounts a generous 1 TB of storage, so running out of space is rarely the reason people back up Yahoo Mail. The more common reasons are:

  • Yahoo has been sold and restructured multiple times, and product changes or account security incidents have made long-time users understandably cautious about relying on it as the only copy of their email history.
  • Migrating away from Yahoo to a different provider, and wanting a local copy before making the switch.
  • Simply wanting an offline, searchable archive that doesn't depend on any provider staying online or keeping your account active.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Yahoo Mail support app passwords?

No. Yahoo Mail does not offer app-specific passwords the way Gmail or iCloud do. If a backup tool asks you to generate one specifically for Yahoo, that guidance is outdated — connect using your regular Yahoo sign-in through the client's standard IMAP connection flow instead.

What are Yahoo's IMAP settings?

IMAP server imap.mail.yahoo.com on port 993 with SSL/TLS. For sending mail, SMTP server smtp.mail.yahoo.com on port 465 or 587 with SSL/TLS.

Why would I back up Yahoo Mail if it already gives 1 TB of free storage?

Storage limits aren't usually the issue with Yahoo. The common reasons are wanting an offline archive independent of any one provider, preparing to migrate to a different email service, or simply wanting peace of mind after Yahoo's history of ownership changes and past account security incidents.

Can I still use IMAP with Yahoo Mail in 2026?

Yes. Yahoo continues to support standard IMAP and SMTP access for third-party mail clients through its normal sign-in flow.
